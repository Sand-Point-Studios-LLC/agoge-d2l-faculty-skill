After you import: the checklist

The tool cannot see inside D2L. These checks are yours.

Do these three now

  1. Open one question in every quiz, as a student sees it. A quiz can import with blank questions that still show their answers.
  2. Check every due date against your syllabus, in the order a student would meet them.
  3. Turn LockDown Browser and Monitor back on for any quiz that used them. They do not travel in a package.

Then, before students arrive

  1. Open a page with an image and a page with a video. A broken image is easy to miss until a student reports it.
  2. Re-add FAQ and Links to the navbar if you used them. Their content may arrive but students cannot reach it.
  3. Tick “send an email copy” on announcements where you want one. It cannot be set by a package.
  4. Check release conditions and special access. We have no evidence a package carries them.
  5. Check the gradebook. Look for the right grading system and weights, and no duplicate columns.
  6. Look at your quality report for anything marked “look at,” and for checks marked “not built yet.”
