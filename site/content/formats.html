Choosing a format for new documents

You choose a default for each kind of document (lesson page, slides, assignment instructions, handout, syllabus). By default the agent also checks with you each time it creates something new: “I’ll make this an HTML page, as you usually do for lesson pages. Okay, or another format?” You can switch that check off and let it simply use your default. You can change your mind at any time.

What the kit can make today

HTML is the one format it can write directly. Word, PowerPoint and PDF need extra programs and the kit has no builder for them yet. (It can find and change text inside Word and PowerPoint files you already have; it cannot edit a PDF.) If your default is one of those, the agent will say so, keep your choice on file, and offer either HTML now or a draft of the content for you to paste into Word or PowerPoint. It will not quietly switch formats on you.

Say it in your own words: “From now on, make my lesson pages HTML and my slides PowerPoint.” See what the kit checks: formats are remembered, not checked.
