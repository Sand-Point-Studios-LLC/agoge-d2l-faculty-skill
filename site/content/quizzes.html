What you can control on a quiz

None of these has a right answer. They are choices. The agent will walk you through them and save yours.

Attempts
1 is strictest. 2 or 3 let students learn from a miss. Unlimited suits ungraded practice. Decide before the term: a quiz that already has attempts cannot be changed by import.
Time limit, and whether it is enforced
These are two separate boxes. A limit that is not enforced is shown to students and does nothing. The report shows both.
Grace period
A few extra minutes before D2L marks an attempt late.
Forward only
Students cannot go back to earlier questions. Stricter, and some students dislike it.
Answer order (A, B, C, D)
Shuffling stops a bank where the right answer is always listed first from showing up at A. It is a per-question box, so a quiz can be half shuffled. The audit reports it.
Question order and banks
Random order of the questions themselves is a separate setting. A bank lets you draw, say, 15 from 25, so students get different quizzes. The draw cannot be larger than the bank.
Annotation tools
Students can bookmark and strike through answers while taking the quiz.
Extra results view
A second view that opens later (for example the morning after the quiz closes) and shows questions, correct answers and the student’s own responses. The default view shows the grade only.
Class average and score distribution
Can be shown to students. Off is the usual choice for a graded quiz.
Calendar entry
Puts the due date in the D2L calendar.
LockDown Browser and Monitor
Not carried by a package. Turn them back on by hand.
Opens, due and closes
D2L has three dates. Late attempts are accepted between due and closes.

Name a quiz for what it covers (“Quiz – Lessons 4 and 5”), not by number.
