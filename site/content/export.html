Export your course from D2L

  1. Open the course you want to copy forward.
  2. Click Course Admin in the navbar, then Import / Export / Copy Components.
  3. Choose Export Components, then Start.
  4. Choose Select Components and tick what you want carried forward: Content, Dropbox, Quizzes, Question Library, News (announcements), Grades, Calendar.
  5. If a box mentions students, user data, progress or submissions, leave it unticked.
  6. On the last screen, tick “Include course files.” Without it you get a tiny export of about 1 MB that shows the course outline and none of the page content.
  7. Click Continue, then Export. When it says finished, click Download. Do not unzip it.
Put the zip in the put-export-here folder of your Roll-Forward folder. A full export can be 700 MB or more. That is normal.

A D2L course export carries the course’s structure and settings, not student work. Quiz attempts, submissions and discussion posts never travel in an export, and are not carried by the export. A quiz that already has attempts still cannot be updated by importing (see the pitfalls page).
