Videos from MediaSpace

Upload the video to MediaSpace first. The agent then builds the embed block for your page.

Steps

  1. Go to your school’s MediaSpace (Kaltura) site and sign in. It is usually named mediaspace followed by your school’s web address.
  2. Upload the video, or find one you have already uploaded: your name (top right), then My Media.
  3. Click the video, then Share, then the Embed tab, and copy the embed code. It starts with <div or <iframe.
  4. In the agent, type: “Make an embed block from this code, titled ‘[what the video shows]’:” and paste the code.
  5. Put the block into your page: ask the agent to add it to the page you are working on, or paste it into the page’s HTML source view in D2L.

Give it a real title

The title is what a screen reader announces before the video plays. Say what the video shows, such as “How to read an income statement,” not “Video 3.”

What the tool does not do

  • It cannot see inside MediaSpace. Open your page as a student and play the video. Check that captions are on and are correct. MediaSpace can generate captions; you review them.
  • It builds the block from the code you paste. Building from an entry ID alone is less certain, because we have not confirmed that a per-video widget setting is optional. Pasting the full code is the safe route.
  • It refuses to build a block from an iframe that does not point at MediaSpace or Kaltura.
