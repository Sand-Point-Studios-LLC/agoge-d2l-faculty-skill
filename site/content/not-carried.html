What an export does not carry

Check these by hand in D2L after any import.

LockDown Browser and Monitor
Turn them back on. D2L also renames a LockDown quiz, so an import never matches it by name.
Release conditions and special access
We have no evidence an export carries them.
The navbar, and so FAQ and Links
Their content may arrive but students cannot reach it until you add the link back.
Homepage widgets and course settings
The settings file in an export is minimal.
Announcement “send an email copy”
No setting exists for it in the format. Tick it when you post.
Anything about people
Enrolments, grades, quiz attempts, submissions and discussion posts.
Publisher tool pairings (LTI)
A link you recreate is not paired and will not return grades. Move the existing one.
