Privacy and student data

What is sent. The text the agent reads from your files, and the text it prints, is sent to the AI company behind your assistant (OpenAI for ChatGPT, Anthropic for Claude) under your account so it can answer. That includes quiz questions, answer keys, rubrics and instructor-only pages whenever the agent opens them. Your files are stored on your own computer, but nothing you let the agent read is private from the AI.

Before you use it

  1. Check your institution’s guidance for the kind of material you are working with. Many schools have a policy or an approved list for AI tools. We cannot tell you what yours allows, so ask your IT or privacy office if you work with protected material.
  2. Publisher content. Some publishers forbid putting their test banks or answer keys into third-party AI tools. If your course uses licensed banks, check the licence before the agent opens them.
  3. Use the account your institution provides, if it provides one, not a personal one. Institutional accounts often carry data-protection terms that personal accounts do not.
  4. You remain responsible for what you import into your course.

Student information

  • A D2L course export carries structure and settings. Quiz attempts, submissions and discussion posts are not part of it. But course files, announcements and discussion topics can still hold names (a guest-speaker roster, a pasted email, a team list).
  • When you export, leave unticked any box that mentions students, user data, progress or submissions.
  • The tool looks for obvious student-data files by file name and stops if it finds one. It does not read inside every file, so it is a safety net, not a guarantee.
  • If you suspect a file holds names, tell the agent not to open it, or remove it and export again.

What stays on your computer

  • Your export, the unpacked copy, the rebuilt package and the reports.
  • The kit never logs in to D2L. Nothing it does can change your live course.

What the agent may look up on the web

For the academic calendar, the agent searches the web. It should send only the calendar question, not your course text. If you want certainty, paste the calendar page into the chat yourself and tell it not to search.
