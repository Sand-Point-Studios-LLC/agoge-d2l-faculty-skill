About this site and the reports

The pages and reports are written to meet WCAG 2.1 AA as far as we have checked: headings, landmarks, a skip link, keyboard focus, and status shown in words as well as symbols. They have not had a full screen-reader review. If something here is hard to use, tell Teagan Dixon.
