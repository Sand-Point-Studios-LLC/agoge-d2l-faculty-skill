Dates and the academic calendar

The rule

The agent does not give you term dates from memory. It looks them up on your school’s official academic calendar (usually the Registrar’s page), shows you the first day, last day, finals and breaks with the link and today’s date, and waits for you to confirm.

In our test, Codex reached the web and returned a full set of term dates with the link to the calendar it used. Confirm anything the agent finds against your own Registrar’s calendar. If it cannot reach the web, paste the calendar text into the chat. Tell the agent the name of your school the first time, so it searches the right calendar.

How the shift works

  • Moving by whole weeks keeps every weekday the same. The tool warns you if the shift is not a whole number of weeks.
  • Times stay put across daylight saving, so 11:59 PM stays 11:59 PM.
  • Dates written in sentences are not moved. The quality report lists the pages that have them.

Things to check by hand

  • Break weeks and finals week. Finals often run Monday to Friday, not Sunday.
  • If your sections meet on different days, a holiday takes a different meeting from each.
  • Whether every item still opens, is due and closes when you intend.
